What it is

A thinking partner on our own hardware

Many AI setups are a wrapper around a remote API. EVAA runs on our own hardware, with three local models for three jobs: language (qwen3:8b), speech recognition (Whisper small) and voice (Piper). Cloud AI models are optional. Features that need the internet (web search, weather, markets, update checks) contact outside services.

EVE Systems is the locally hosted software platform EVAA runs on: identity, governance, memory and protocol layers. In-house; built on open-source foundations. Messages inside it use AACP™, a structured message protocol with a fixed format for every message.
